Our story

Poppy at Heart

Inspired by the wild red poppies that spring up along the hillsides each summer.

We take local favorites and transform them into French and Japanese style crêpes on the griddle and topped with cream and gelato. Modern, playful, and made to order — come say hi!

We’re women-owned and homegrown. Poppy Crêpes & Café was started by a local — who traded a career in AI and big tech for the warmth of baking — bringing the craft back home.
